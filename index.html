Level control that protects your plant

Accumulator with Automatic Level Controller

By installing an automatic level controller, it offers the following benefits:

  1. Protects the compressor by preventing liquid refrigerant from entering it, reducing the risk of damage.
  2. Saves electricity by improving the system's overall efficiency.
  3. Speeds up ice production for faster ice-making cycles.
  4. Reduces maintenance with automatic operation and less manual monitoring.
